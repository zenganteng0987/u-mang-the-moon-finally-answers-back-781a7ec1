One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it. `main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout.

Entry: "I heard every wish, every howl, every 2 a.m. promise — after four billion years, my answer is still no, and I'm keeping the lottery numbers."
